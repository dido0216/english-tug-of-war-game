🪢 English Tug of War Games

Play, Learn, Create!

Two tug-of-war games for Grade 7 English classes in Taiwan — the same 58 questions, two different adventures. Pick the style that fits your classroom!

⭐ TAKE TURNS

🪄 Magic Academy

Enter a wizard school at dusk! Azure Star House (blue) battles Crimson Phoenix House (red) to pull the Crystal of Light into their rune circle.

  • Teams answer one at a time — great for teacher-led lessons on a projector
  • Modes: Classic (15 pulls), Sudden Spell (5 pulls), Time Attack (60 seconds)
  • Young wizards, floating candles and an owl professor
▶ Play Magic Academy
⭐ BOTH TEAMS TOGETHER

🏝️ Adventure Island

A sunny island quest! Team Sky (blue) vs Team Berry (pink), with cute animal buddies — fox, bunny, panda, puppy, kitty, lion, frog and unicorn.

  • Both teams play at the same time — made for interactive displays and touch boards: each team taps its own answers on its own side
  • Both correct? Both score — and the fastest tap pulls the rope!
  • Quest modes: Rainbow Trek, Lightning Trail, River Race
▶ Play Adventure Island

🤔 Which one should I use?

Projector + one classroom computer? Choose Magic Academy — the teacher runs the game and teams take turns.
Big interactive touch display? Choose Adventure Island — students from both teams stand at the board and tap their answers together. Nobody waits!
