🎮 How to play — both teams at the same time! Stand on your side of the big screen. Every question appears in the middle, and both teams tap their own answer on their own side — no waiting for turns! Tapping locks your answer in 🔒. Only one team right? That team pulls the rope and scores +1. Both teams right? Both score +1, and the fastest tap pulls the rope! Nobody right? No pull — look at the green answer and learn it together. First team to pull the flag into their goal circle wins the round. Match Wins stay for rematches!

👩‍🏫 For Teachers — Publish on Google Sites & Edit Questions

Embed in Google Sites: 1) Host this single index.html file (e.g. GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, or your school web space). 2) In Google Sites choose Insert → Embed → URL, paste the hosted address, and set it to full width. 3) Publish — students can play on computers, tablets, or the classroom interactive display, with both teams tapping their own side of the screen at the same time. No account or server is needed, and the game works offline after it loads.

Edit questions: Open index.html in a text editor, find the QUESTION_BANK array in the script, and copy any question block to add your own: cat, diff, q, zh, options (4), answer (0–3), explain.
